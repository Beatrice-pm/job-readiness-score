How to improve your score

    How is this score calculated?
    • Skills (25 pts) — how many specific, real skills you listed (up to 5 counted)
    • Experience (25 pts) — scaled by years of relevant work experience
    • Education (15 pts) — capped low on purpose, since many good jobs don't require a degree
    • Profile completeness (20 pts) — whether every question was answered with real, specific detail
    • Application readiness (15 pts) — whether you have a CV/LinkedIn ready to actually send

    This is a simple, transparent rules-based score — not an AI judgment of your career. It rewards a complete, specific profile.
